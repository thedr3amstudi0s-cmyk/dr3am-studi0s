About Us

—
members have joined DR3AM STUDI0S

DR3AM STUDI0S is the passion project of Dreamcrusher, a solo game developer who loves creating fun, competitive games and challenging puzzles. With a focus on exciting gameplay experiences, the studio's mission is to make games that are engaging, memorable, and just plain fun to play.

So far, DR3AM STUDI0S has released one game, and there's an exciting project on the horizon: The Experiment — a competitive puzzle-adventure that pushes players to think strategically while testing their reflexes.

Every game is designed with care, creativity, and a focus on the player experience. By supporting DR3AM STUDI0S through feedback, donations, or simply following the studio's journey, you're helping make these games even better.
